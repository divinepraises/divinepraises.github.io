God be merciful to us, and bless us;*
Make the light of Your face shine upon us and have mercy on us.•
 That we may know Your way upon the earth,*
Your salvation among all nations.•
 Let the peoples give praise to You, O God,*
may all the peoples give praise to You.•
 Let the nations rejoice and exult,*
For You shall judge the peoples in equity,
And shall guide the nations on the earth.•
 Let the peoples give praise to You, O God,*
may all the peoples give praise to You.•
 The earth has yielded her fruit;*
O God, our God, bless us.•
 O God, bless us;*
And let all the ends of the earth fear Him.•
